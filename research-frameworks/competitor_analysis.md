# Competitor Analysis Framework

The structure I use when a founder asks "who else does this, and how do we win?". It keeps the focus on the **problem and the use case**; data goes in an appendix for reference.

## 1. One-line problem statement
What customer problem are we comparing solutions for?

## 2. Competitor map
| Company | Who they sell to | Core use case | Pricing model | Strength | Weakness | Threat (H/M/L) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Group competitors into **direct** (same problem, same buyer), **indirect** (same problem, different approach) and **substitutes** (spreadsheets, agencies, doing nothing).

## 3. Where we win / where we lose
| Buying criterion (what the customer cares about) | Us | Competitor A | Competitor B |
|---|---|---|---|

## 4. So what?
- 2–3 specific recommendations (positioning, features, target segment)
- What we should **not** do

## Appendix
Sources, funding data, pricing pages, screenshots (reference only).
