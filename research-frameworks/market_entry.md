# Market Entry Framework

Used to answer: *"Should we enter [sector], and where do we start?"*

1. **Problem statement**: what's broken in the sector, and for whom?
2. **Sub-segments**: break the sector into parts (e.g. finance → lending, markets, insurance, PE/IB).
3. **Score each sub-segment**
   | Sub-segment | Pain severity | Fit with our product | Ease of access to buyers | Regulation burden | Competition | Total |
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
4. **Pick the beachhead**: the top-scoring sub-segment and one specific use case.
5. **First 5 target customers** and who the buyer is (title).
6. **Risks & unknowns** to validate in customer conversations.
