# Scorecard: Business & Product roles: Associate / Manager (First Round)

**Candidate:** ____________  **Date:** ________  **Interviewer:** ________  **Duration:** ___ min

Covers **Business & Product Associate** and **Product & Business Operations Manager**. Same questions, different bar:

| Level | What a "3 – Meets" answer looks like |
|---|---|
| **Associate** (0–3 yrs) | Clear example from an internship, college project or first job; structured thinking matters more than scale of impact |
| **Manager** (2–7 yrs) | Owned the outcome, with measurable results; led people or processes across teams |

**Level being hired:** ☐ Associate ☐ Manager

---

## 0. Opening (3 min)
Introduce yourself and the company in two lines, then explain the format: "about 30 minutes of questions, then 5–10 minutes for yours."

## 1. Motivation & context (5 min)
- Walk me through your career so far in 2 minutes. Why each move?
- Why a small startup now, and why this role?

| Look for ✅ | Red flags 🚩 |
|---|---|
| Clear reasons for each move; can name what they want to learn here | Only salary or title as the reason; vague about current role |

## 2. Product sense (8 min)
- Tell me about a product or feature you helped ship. What problem did it solve, and how did you know it worked?
- *Follow-up:* What did you cut to ship on time? What metric moved?

| ✅ | 🚩 |
|---|---|
| Starts from the user problem; names a metric; owns trade-offs | Describes only tasks ("I made the PRD"); no outcome |
